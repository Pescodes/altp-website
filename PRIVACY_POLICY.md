# Privacy Policy for Alt+P Autofill

**Effective Date: February 28, 2026**

Alt+P Autofill ("Extension") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the Extension.

By installing or using the Extension, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Data Collection and Use

Alt+P Autofill is designed to operate without transmitting your personal autofill data to external servers.

### Local Storage Only

All profile information you enter (such as name, email, social media handles, or other form data):

- Is stored locally in your browser
- May sync across your own Google Chrome account using the `chrome.storage.sync` API
- Is not accessible to the Developer
- Is not transmitted to external servers by the Extension

This data is used solely to provide autofill functionality.

### Limited Processing Activities

The following limited data processing may occur:

- **License Verification:** The Extension may communicate with Paddle to verify license validity.
- **Support Communication:** If you contact us via email, we will process your email address and message content for support purposes.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data.

---

## 2. Payments and Third-Party Services

Payments for premium licenses are processed by Paddle, which acts as the Merchant of Record.

When you make a purchase, Paddle may collect and process personal information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Billing address
- Payment details

This information is processed by Paddle in accordance with their Privacy Policy.

Alt+P Autofill does not collect or store payment information.

---

## 3. Permissions and Their Purpose

The Extension requests permissions strictly to enable functionality:

**Storage**  
To securely store your saved profile information.

**ContextMenus**  
To enable right-click autofill options.

**ActiveTab and Scripting**  
To insert saved data into form fields on the page you choose.

**Host Permissions**  
To detect and autofill form fields on supported websites.

The Extension does not:

- Track browsing history  
- Monitor website activity  
- Record keystrokes  
- Capture page content for storage  
- Transmit browsing data externally  

---

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable under data protection laws (including GDPR and NDPA), data processing is based on:

- Contractual necessity (to provide the Extension and license verification)
- Legitimate interests (to maintain product functionality and security)
- Legal obligations (where required)

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your stored data.

### Manual Deletion
You may clear stored data at any time using the "Clear" button within the Extension.

### Uninstallation
Uninstalling the Extension removes locally stored data from your browser.

Support emails are retained only as long as necessary to resolve inquiries or comply with legal obligations.

The Developer does not maintain backups of locally stored autofill data.
