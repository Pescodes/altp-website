# Privacy Policy for AltP Autofill

**Effective Date:** February 28, 2026

AltP Autofill ("Extension") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Extension handles user data.

By installing or using the Extension, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Data Collection and Use

AltP Autofill does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or store user data on external servers.

### Local Storage Only

All profile information you enter (such as name, email, social media handles, or other form data):

- Is stored locally in your browser
- May sync across your own Google Chrome account using the `chrome.storage.sync` API
- Is not accessible to the developer
- Is not transmitted to any third-party servers

The data is used solely to provide autofill functionality.

---

## 2. Permissions and Their Purpose

AltP Autofill requests the following permissions strictly to enable its functionality:

### Storage
To securely store your saved profile information.

### ContextMenus
To allow right-click autofill options within supported form fields.

### ActiveTab and Scripting
To inject your saved data into form fields on the specific webpage you choose.

### Host Permissions
The Extension may request access to website pages in order to detect and autofill form fields.

The Extension does **not**:

- Track browsing history
- Monitor website activity
- Record keystrokes
- Capture page content for storage
- Send browsing data externally

Access to web pages is used exclusively to perform autofill actions initiated by the user.

---

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data.

### Manual Deletion
You may clear all stored data at any time using the "Clear" button within the extension popup.

### Uninstallation
Uninstalling the Extension will remove all associated locally stored data from your browser.

The developer does not maintain backups and cannot recover deleted data.

---

## 4. Payments and Third-Party Services

If you purchase a premium version, payment processing is handled by Paddle, which acts as the Merchant of Record.

Paddle may collect payment-related information in accordance with its own privacy policy.

AltP Autofill does not collect or store payment details.

---

## 5. Data Security

Data is stored using Chrome’s built-in storage APIs and protected by the security of your browser and Google account.

You are responsible for securing:

- Your device
- Your browser
- Your Google account credentials

The developer is not responsible for data loss due to:

- Device compromise
- Unauthorized account access
- Browser corruption
- Sync errors
